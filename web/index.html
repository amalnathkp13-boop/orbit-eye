Validation against the test plan

Every number below comes from the engine running in this page, exercised by a scripted test suite: 20-cycle bidirectional repeatability, 1000 randomised moves with random patient offsets, 115 injected faults, 40 e-stops at peak speed, hand-over force steps, patient leans and a head-mode sweep. The model covers motor and load inertia, ball-screw and harmonic-drive stiffness, backlash, stiction, encoder quantisation and noise, a cantilevered head mode, depth-camera noise and breathing.

Not modelled: thermal drift, rail straightness, encoder scale error, cable drag and frame flex. Expect a real build to land at 5–15 µm repeatability instead of the 2 µm the model predicts.
